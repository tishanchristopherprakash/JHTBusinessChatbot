SECTION: COMPANY OVERVIEW
================================================================================
 
JHT Cleaning Service is a professional cleaning company based in Ampang, Selangor, Malaysia. The company was founded by Shyamala Deenathayalan during the COVID-19 pandemic, when she identified a critical need for professional sanitation and hygiene services during the lockdowns. JHT started as a small one-person door-to-door service and grew into a structured corporate entity with dedicated office locations in Ampang and Kuala Lumpur City Centre.
 
JHT Cleaning Service has been operating for approximately five years and has served over 1,000 satisfied clients since its inception. A significant portion of JHT's business comes from word-of-mouth referrals and recurring contracts for office and building maintenance. JHT Cleaning Service was recognised as one of the Top 10 Best Commercial Cleaning Services in Kuala Lumpur by Malaysia Brands, selected based on quality of service and real customer reviews.
